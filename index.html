In a busy old-age home, routines deserve backup. PillMate creates a clear, repeatable cue for every scheduled dose—helping residents and caregivers move through the day with more confidence.

01

Clear cue

The device makes the moment obvious through light, an opening compartment, and a persistent sound.

02

Focused dose

Only the scheduled space is highlighted, helping make a multi-medicine routine feel less overwhelming.

03

Shared calm

Residents retain a sense of independence, while helpers have a consistent physical prompt to rely on.

PillMate is designed to support medication routines and should be used alongside professional care guidance and the instructions provided for each medicine.
